About us

Our story

Why we sift, what the rooms are made of, and where to find us.

The idea

A cafe worth staying a little longer in.

Bakers sift flour to take out the lumps and keep what is fine. We borrowed the habit for the day: leave the hurry at the door, keep the good parts.

The Sifted is a cafe and a bakery under one roof. Espresso is pulled at the bar, bakes come out of the oven, and the kitchen sends out plates for those who plan to sit awhile.

We furnished the rooms the way a good home gets furnished, slowly and without matching sets. Warm lamps, vintage chairs, a shelf of records and a typewriter make every corner a little different, so there is always a spot that suits the mood you came in with.

  • Wood-panelled coffee bar
  • Mismatched vintage chairs
  • Vinyl records and a turntable
  • Paper lanterns and amber lamps
Our locations

Two branches, two personalities.

01

Calamba

Chipeco Avenue, Brgy. 3, Calamba

Our Calamba branch sits on Chipeco Avenue, close to the center of the city. It is our more compact branch, and the one to pick for a coffee between errands, a long lunch with friends, or an afternoon with a laptop and a second cup.

02

Cabuyao

South Point, Pulo, Cabuyao

In Cabuyao we are on the second floor of Southwalk Commercial Space in South Point, a step above the street. It is much roomier than Calamba. Come up for a slower table, a catch-up that is allowed to run long, or a quiet hour before the day starts.
